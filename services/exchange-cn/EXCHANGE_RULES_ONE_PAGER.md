# How an exchange is valued

**Two options for the customer**

- **Full Value:** the customer gets back exactly what they were charged for that piece, GST included.
- **Deduction:** the piece is revalued at today's prices:
  - gold at 100% of today's rate for its karat;
  - diamond at 80% of today's catalogue price;
  - nothing for making, gemstones, shipping, IGI or GST.

**The diamond cap**

The diamond credit can never exceed what the customer actually paid for the stone. Usually this changes nothing. It matters when catalogue diamond prices have risen sharply or a big discount came off the stone.

*Example (#1045):* the catalogue diamond price nearly tripled since April. 80% of today's price would credit ₹29,600, but the customer paid ₹12,759.59 for the stone, so the credit is ₹12,759.59.

**When an old order is missing information**

Each gap has a fixed rule that can only err in Timanti's favour. No manual approval is needed.

| Gap | Rule |
|---|---|
| Stone price not recorded, no discount | The diamond price on the order is the stone price |
| Discount given, split between stone and making not recorded | The stone is taken at the lowest it could have cost |
| No price breakup at all | The credit can never exceed what was paid for the piece, excluding GST |
| Weight not recorded | The catalogue weight is used, or staff weigh the piece |
| The variant sold has since been recreated | The current listing with the same SKU code is used |

**When the sheet refuses**

The sheet refuses to issue anything for:

- test orders;
- cancelled or refunded orders;
- items already exchanged or returned;
- any figure edited by hand after the lookup.

Wherever a fallback rule was used, staff must confirm before issuing, and that confirmation is logged.

**Status of vouchers and exchange notes**

Statuses (applied, redeemed, voided, expired) come from the central ledger and are refreshed into the sheet daily at 12:00 and 18:00 IST.
