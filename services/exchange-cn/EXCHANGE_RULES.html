How an exchange is valued

The rules the Exchange Calculator follows when a customer brings back a piece, what it does when an old order is missing information, and when it refuses to issue anything.

The two ways to exchange

Deduction

The piece is revalued at today's prices.

  • Gold at 100% of today's rate for its karat
  • Diamond at 80% of today's catalogue price, never more than the customer paid for the stone
  • Nothing for making, gemstones, shipping, IGI or GST

Full Value

The customer gets back what they were charged for that piece, GST included.

  • Taken from what Shopify recorded as charged for the piece
  • Today's prices play no part
  • For a multi-piece order, only the piece being returned counts

Any custom deduction staff enter (always a negative number) comes off the final figure in both modes.

The Deduction formula

Credit = net gold weight × today's rate for that karat + the smaller of ( 80% × today's diamond price , what was paid for the stone )

The second part is the diamond cap. On most orders it changes nothing, because 80% of the diamond price is already below what the customer paid. It only matters when the catalogue diamond price has risen a lot since the sale, or when a large discount came off the stone. Without it, an exchange could credit the stone at more than the customer ever paid for it.

Where each number comes from

Gold rate
Today's rate for the piece's karat, from the same gold-rate setting the repricing job uses (14K and 18K have different rates). If that setting cannot be read, the product's own rate is used and the sheet says so.
Net gold weight
In order of preference: the weight recorded on the order; the gold charged divided by the rate recorded on the order; the catalogue weight; a weight staff measure at the counter, which needs a written reason.
Catalogue weights have been edited since some sales, so using one always asks staff to confirm, and they can weigh the piece instead.
Today's diamond price
The catalogue price of the listing the piece was sold as.
Which listing
The exact variant sold. If Shopify has since given it a new ID, the listing with the same SKU code in the same tone, karat and size. Only if no listing with that code exists does it fall back to another listing on the same product, and then staff are told it is a different code.
#1044 and #1050: the products are live, but the variant IDs on the orders no longer exist because the variants were recreated.
Silver
100% of the metal at today's catalogue silver price. The system has no daily silver rate.
#1055 silver coin: 5 g × ₹290 = ₹1,450.

When an old order is missing information

Orders from before August 2026 often lack part of the price breakup. Each gap is settled by a fixed rule that can only err in Timanti's favour, and the sheet tells staff which rule it used. No one has to approve anything.

What was paid for the stone is not recorded, but there was no discount
The diamond price on the order is what was paid.
Orders up to April 2026
The prices on these orders are already after discount, so the diamond price on the order is what was paid for the stone, and the discount is not subtracted again.
Recognised by checking which reading adds up to the amount actually charged.
A discount was given, but not how it was split between stone and making
The stone is taken at the least it could have cost: as if the whole discount came off the stone.
#1056: diamond ₹65,400 − ₹4,822 discount = ₹60,578 cap.
The breakup is slightly off the amount charged
The reading that lands within 1% of the charge is used.
#1039: ₹449.94 off (0.7%).
No breakup at all
The stone can't have cost more than the piece's price excluding GST, minus its gold, so that is the cap. The whole Deduction is also held to the price excluding GST.
#1041 bangle: today's catalogue values it at ₹95,245 of gold alone, against ₹57,225 paid excluding GST. Credit: ₹57,225.
Custom item with no listing
Staff enter the catalogue SKU it corresponds to, with a reason, and it is valued as that SKU. Otherwise only Full Value is available.

Voucher and exchange note status

Whether a voucher or exchange note has been applied, redeemed, voided or has expired is known only to the ledger the middleware keeps. The sheet copies that onto the After-Sales Log, the Voucher Log and the Exchange Log every day at 12:00 and 18:00 IST, and whenever someone runs Refresh log statuses from ledger from the menu. Voided rows are never changed.

Before this, the Voucher and Exchange Logs kept the status written at issue. That is why EXC27-KAHSR-0001 read "Applied" although it was redeemed against #D154 on 1 July.
