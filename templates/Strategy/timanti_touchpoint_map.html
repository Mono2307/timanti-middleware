Staff Action Reference
TAG in-store-sale — Add to the Shopify order before finalizing a walk-in payment. Controls email content and suppresses shipping sections.
SEND Draft Order Invoice (advance receipt) — Not automatic. After GoKwik payment success, open the Shopify draft order → click Send invoice.
SEND Shipping Confirmation — Not automatic. When marking fulfilled in Shopify, check Send shipment details to customer now before clicking Fulfill.
WA WhatsApp follow-up — Sent manually between GoKwik link generation and payment. Resend the link and ask for confirmation. Use the WhatsApp template on file.
